## 听力理解（Listening — 20题）

### L1
男人说"Good morning. My name is Tom. I'm from London." 问题：这个男人叫什么名字？
- 选项：A. Mike B. Tom C. John
- 正确答案：B. Tom

### L2
女人说"I would like a cup of coffee, please." 问题：这个女人想要什么？
- 选项：A. Tea B. Coffee C. Juice
- 正确答案：B. Coffee

### L3
男人说"The book is on the table." 问题：书在哪里？
- 选项：A. On the chair B. On the table C. Under the bed
- 正确答案：B. On the table

### L4

- 选项：A. One B. Two C. Three
- 正确答案：B. Two

### L5
男人说"It's 3 o'clock. Time to go home." 问题：现在几点了？
- 选项：A. 2:00 B. 3:00 C. 4:00
- 正确答案：B. 3:00

### L6

- 选项：A. By car B. By bus C. On foot

### L7

### L8

### L9

- 选项：A. 6:30 PM B. 8:00 PM C. 10:00 PM

### L10

- 选项：A. He has a fever B. He has a headache C. He has a stomachache

### L11

### L12

### L13

- 选项：A. It's more convenient B. It's faster C. It's more fun

### L14
男人说"The company is looking for someone with at least five years of experience." 问题：公司要求几年的工作经验？
- 选项：A. Three years B. Five years C. Ten years

### L15
女人说"Although I was tired, I decided to finish the report before going to bed." 问题：女人什么时候做完报告？
- 选项：A. Before going to bed B. After going to bed C. In the morning

### L16

### L17

## 口语表达（Speaking — 15题）

- 评分维度：表达完整性

### S2

- 评分维度：词汇量

### S3

- 评分维度：表达连贯性

### S4
请说出你最喜欢的颜色并用英语表达。
- 评分维度：基础表达

### S5
请描述你的家庭成员（至少3人）。
- 评分维度：表达完整性

### S6

- 评分维度：过去时态使用

### S7
请说出你最喜欢的食物并说明原因。
- 评分维度：观点表达

### S8
请描述你住的房子或公寓。
- 评分维度：描述能力

### S9
请用英语介绍一个你喜欢的节日并说明为什么喜欢。
- 评分维度：观点+理由

### S10
请描述一次你难忘的旅行经历。
- 评分维度：叙述能力

### S11

- 评分维度：日常表达

### S12

- 评分维度：观点+理由

### S13

- 评分维度：论述能力

### S14
请用英语表达你对"远程办公"的看法。
- 评分维度：论述能力

### S15
请用英语描述一个你曾经面对的挑战并说明你是如何应对的。
- 评分维度：叙述+反思

## 阅读理解（Reading — 11题）

### R1
阅读："My name is Anna. I am 25. I live in New York." 问题：Anna住在哪里？
- 选项：A. London B. New York C. Paris
- 正确答案：B. New York

### R2
阅读："Tom has a dog. The dog's name is Max." 问题：Tom有什么宠物？
- 选项：A. A cat B. A dog C. A bird
- 正确答案：B. A dog

### R3
阅读："The shop opens at 9 and closes at 6." 问题：商店几点开门？
- 选项：A. 8 B. 9 C. 10
- 正确答案：B. 9

### R4
阅读："I like apples. I don't like oranges." 问题：这个人喜欢什么？
- 选项：A. Oranges B. Apples C. Bananas
- 正确答案：B. Apples

### R5
阅读："Sarah works in a hospital. She is a nurse." 问题：Sarah做什么工作？
- 选项：A. A teacher B. A nurse C. A doctor
- 正确答案：B. A nurse

### R6
阅读："There are four seasons. I like summer because I can go swimming." 问题：作者为什么喜欢夏天？
- 选项：A. Because he can go swimming B. Because it's cold C. Because he can ski
- 正确答案：A. Because he can go swimming

### R7
阅读："Many people enjoy traveling to other countries. When you travel, you can learn about different cultures." 问题：人们为什么喜欢旅行？
- 选项：A. To learn about cultures B. To spend money C. To stay home
- 正确答案：A. To learn about cultures

### R8
阅读："Many people enjoy traveling. However, traveling can also be expensive." 问题：旅行的缺点是什么？
- 选项：A. It's boring B. It's expensive C. It's dangerous
- 正确答案：B. It's expensive

### R9
阅读："Scientists agree that human activities are contributing to global warming." 问题：气候变化的主要原因是什么？
- 选项：A. Natural disasters B. Human activities C. Animal behavior
- 正确答案：B. Human activities

### R10
阅读："Many countries are investing in renewable energy sources like solar and wind power." 问题：许多国家正在投资什么？
- 选项：A. Fossil fuels B. Renewable energy C. Nuclear power
- 正确答案：B. Renewable energy

### R11
阅读："To reduce the impact, many countries are investing in renewable energy sources like solar and wind power." 问题：文中提到的可再生能源有哪些？
- 选项：A. Solar and wind B. Coal and gas C. Oil and nuclear
- 正确答案：A. Solar and wind
